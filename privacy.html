Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

SoundWave ("we") runs music promotion campaigns with TikTok creators through our Discord community. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your Discord ID and display name when you join our server.
  • The social accounts you link to take part in campaigns, and the clips you submit.
  • Your payout email, used only to pay you.
  • If you choose to connect TikTok: read-only access to your profile (username, avatar, follower and like counts), your video list, and the analytics TikTok provides for your videos (views, likes, shares, watch time, and anonymous audience percentages such as viewer countries and gender).

What we never do

We never post, upload, draft, comment, or change anything on your TikTok account, and we don't request permission to. We can't see your messages or password. We never sell your data.

How we use it

  • To confirm you own the accounts you submit clips from.
  • To track campaign views and calculate payouts.
  • To show artists and labels aggregated campaign reports, such as total views and the share of views by country.

Sharing

Campaign reports shared with the artist or label behind a campaign show your clips and their results. We share data with service providers that help us run SoundWave (hosting, databases, and the TikTok connection provider), only as needed to operate the service.

Storage and security

Data is stored in secured databases with restricted access. TikTok access tokens are never shown or shared.

Your choices

Connecting TikTok is optional. You can disconnect anytime in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions) or by asking us. Email us to see or delete your data and we'll handle it within 30 days.
